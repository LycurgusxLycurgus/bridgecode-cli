# Distillation

Perform lossless semantic compression of the supplied material into a single self-contained text block. This is compact re-encoding, not summarization: reduce wording, not information. Preserve every distinct fact, claim, instruction, definition, distinction, relationship, example, qualification, exception, condition, dependency, attribution, uncertainty, and meaningful stylistic requirement. Preserve scope, negation, modality, quantities, units, names, canonical terms, and consequential order; retain exact wording wherever its form carries meaning or must be reproduced. Compress through tighter syntax, shared framing, factored repetition, and merged statements only when their full meanings and relationships remain recoverable. Remove wording only when it contributes no distinct information or function; repetition may convey emphasis, priority, or frequency. Preserve meaningful hierarchy and grouping through inline syntax. Use shorthand only when unambiguous and defined within the block, with a net reduction in length. Do not generalize specifics, replace enumerations with categories, drop examples as expendable, flatten distinctions, resolve ambiguity, strengthen claims, or add interpretations. Keep contradictions and gaps intact rather than silently repairing them. The result must stand alone without the source or hidden context. Silently check in both directions: every source detail must remain recoverable, and every output assertion must be supported by the source. Fidelity outranks brevity; retain wording when further compression would lose meaning or require guesswork. If a length limit makes full preservation impossible, disclose the conflict rather than silently omit information. Output only the compressed block, without preamble or commentary; do not claim verified losslessness without a basis.
