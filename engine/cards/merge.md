Merging: a count never chooses; evidence does.
- Eligibility (the engine computes it): a candidate is eligible only with its own witnessed real run on the targets, CORE first, then all; a regression disqualifies it; the merged result runs on HEAD before its judging (`{{cmd}} run -- <command>`); nothing is merged on reading alone. The engine refuses a judging when a merged delegate's result has no passing run of its own (`{{cmd}} run --cwd <its worktree> -- <command>`).
- Choice (yours): among the eligible, keep the one that fixes the named cause at its origin with the smallest coherent change; prefer removing over adding; prefer construction (allow-lists, closed formats, exact-key binding) over one more detection rule.
- Amalgam: combine candidates only when they are disjoint and run together; a combination is a new candidate, judged again.
- Drafts (DEFINE): merge by the treatments of the define card; keep what traces to the goals, never the union of everything proposed.
- The rejected become evidence: EXPLORE losers are vetoes (`{{cmd}} veto "<mechanism | why | instead>"`); failed hypotheses are falsified premises (`{{cmd}} falsified "<premise | the run that falsified it>"`).
